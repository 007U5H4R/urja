A concept for Bytebeam · Tushar Pathak · September 2026

Fleet owners learn where their money leaked at month end. Urja tells them the next morning.

Urja is an AI munshi for Indian truck owners. It reconciles every trip’s diesel, tolls and kilometres from data Bytebeam-class devices already send, and explains each rupee that doesn’t add up — in Hindi or English, on WhatsApp, with the driver’s side attached.

What vendor research says, to be tested against the field: fuel is 35–45% of a truck’s operating cost, and industry blogs claim 15–20% of fuel spend is lost to theft or bill fraud. Those numbers are directional, not proven.

02 · The owner

10 to 100 trucks, run from a phone.

The owner doesn’t sit in a control room. They think in rupees per trip, live on WhatsApp, and often prefer Hindi. Today a munshi reconciles trips by hand from paper slips, days late. Every competitor built a dashboard for an enterprise fleet manager. Nobody built for this person.

05 · How we’ll know

One metric that proves the promise, one guardrail for when it works too well.

Success metric

₹ recovered per truck per month

Leading signals: share of mornings the owner opens the brief; share of flags acted on within 24 hours.

Guardrail

False-accusation rate under 10%

Flags the driver disputes and the owner accepts as innocent. If Urja works too well, owners blame drivers for sensor glitches and good drivers leave. Watch driver 90-day retention too.

06 · First 90 days

What I’d do if I joined.

  1. Days 1–30

    Sit with 15 fleet owners and their munshis in Jaipur, Kishangarh and Delhi transport hubs. Map how a trip is reconciled today, and what they do when diesel goes missing.

  2. Days 31–60

    Pilot the five rules on 3 fleets’ real CAN data. Measure the false-flag rate before any owner sees a flag.

  3. Days 61–90

    Ship the WhatsApp brief to the pilot owners. Go or no-go on ₹ recovered per truck and the guardrail.

07 · About this prototype

What’s real here and what isn’t.

Sharma Roadlines is fictional, and its 24 trucks and 30 days of trips are simulated. The leakage rules really run on that data, every rupee on screen is computed, and Ask Urja is a live model answering only from it. Nothing here is a Bytebeam product.
